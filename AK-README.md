# 🚀 Hermes Agent

> **Hermes** is an AI-powered Business Operating System designed to automate marketing, sales, operations, customer management, content creation, and business workflows through an advanced multi-agent architecture.

---

## 🌟 Vision

Hermes is built to become an **AI Chief Operating Officer (AI COO)** for businesses.

Instead of being a single chatbot, Hermes orchestrates multiple specialized AI agents that collaborate to execute business operations autonomously while keeping humans in control of critical decisions.

---

* 🧠 Long-term AI Memory

* 📈 Marketing Automation
